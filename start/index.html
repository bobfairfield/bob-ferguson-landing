How you earn, in three levels

Learn Level 1 first. It is all you need to begin. Levels 2 and 3 are here so you can see where the path goes.

Words you will hear

CustomerSomeone who buys Shaklee products from you.

AmbassadorA Member who also builds a Shaklee business, as you are doing.
Points (PV)How Shaklee counts product purchases. In this guide, 500 points is about $750 in customer sales, and 2,000 points is about $3,000.

Director is the key milestone

Directors earn on the volume of everyone on their team, not only the people they enrolled: up to 16% on Level 1 volume and up to 8% on Level 2 volume, with the Director Team Bonus stacked on top of the level bonuses. Directors also receive a 25% product discount instead of 15%, invitations to leadership calls and training, and added reporting tools.

There are two ways to reach Director. One is about $3,000 (2,000 points) in personal customer sales. The other is to become a Senior Ambassador with about $750 (500 points) in customer sales and help three Ambassadors do the same. The simplest path: build 10 loyal customers first, then coach three Ambassadors to do the same.

There is a current Director Accelerator, a $500 shopping spree for becoming a Director in your first full two months. Confirm current terms in your back office.

Part 8

Two simple tools: the checklist and the tracker

How Do You Feel Today?

Nine areas of everyday comfort, each with a Today column and an After 30 Days column.

It is a private, one-page snapshot of how a person feels right now, with a place to mark what has changed thirty days later. Real change is gradual, so it is easy to miss. The checklist makes it visible.

Two ways to use it.

  • As a baseline. Fill it in before starting Vivix+™ and the stack, then again at day 30.
  • As a natural invitation. It comes up in conversation, and you offer it.

It belongs to the person. You do not read it, ask what they marked, or keep a copy. The questions do the real work, not you. If they choose to show you, that is their choice.

Start with your own, the day you begin Vivix+™.

Download the checklist (PDF you can fill in on screen)

Your prospect tracker

A spreadsheet that keeps your conversations organized as they add up. Three tabs do the work, and the rest is for reference.

Prospect Tracker

One row per person: how you met, when you reached out, whether you sent your landing page or walked through it together, and what is next. Current Stage, Priority and days since last contact fill in automatically.

Priority View

A live, self-sorting list. Ambassadors first, then Members, then people you have walked through the page with, so you always see who needs your attention.

Pipeline Summary

Counts by stage, overdue follow-ups, and how many people you have asked for referrals.

A tab called How To Use explains every column. One rule keeps it clean: one row per person, always. If someone comes back after going quiet, update their row.

Part 9

How this grows: community, not a list

You do not need a list of a hundred names or a stream of posts. You belong to communities already, and in each one you carry some trust. This business grows by being useful there, one person at a time.

Where trust already exists

A fitness class, a coaching group, a faith community, a civic club, a school or neighborhood group, a circle of business owners. Joe Weaver's story on the page came from one: a trusted instructor who tried it himself and said so.

Be the one who tried it

Your thirty days, and your own checklist, are the most believable thing you have. Share what you notice when it comes up naturally, not before.

Offer a walk-through

When someone shows interest, do not pitch. Ask, "Would you like me to go through this page with you?" Then follow Part 6.

Let the tools travel for you

Your card puts your page in someone's hand. Your page does the explaining. Your tracker remembers every conversation so you can follow up when the time is right. When a neighbor gets results, their story reaches a community you may never have entered yourself.

What this is

  • Real conversations, one at a time
  • Walk-throughs, virtual or in person
  • Following up with care, and on time

What this is not

  • A list of a hundred friends to work through
  • Mass posting or message blasts
  • Adding health claims of your own. Let the page speak.

Share it the right way

  • Label everything you create as from a "Shaklee Ambassador."
  • Do not claim products diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.
  • Do not make income or lifestyle claims. Never show checks, bonuses, or statements.
  • Link the Average Earnings Statement whenever you talk about the opportunity.
  • Send self-produced product materials to compliance@shaklee.com for written approval before you use them.

The Compliance kit in Shaklee Tools has "Say This, Not That" guides for sharing both products and the opportunity.
